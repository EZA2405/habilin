# Global stuck-estate headlines for the video intro

Checked: 20 October 2026. Purpose: first 45 seconds of the Habilin demo video. Angle: paperwork, delays, idle or frozen assets, unregistered titles, unclaimed inheritances. Not fraud.

Verification method: each URL was loaded by an automated request on 20 Oct 2026 and the page title compared to the headline below. The key sentence was then read from the page text. Exceptions are marked. "Official" means the number traces to a government, regulator or parliamentary source. "Media-reported" means it does not, or the trace could not be confirmed.

## 3. Recommended 45-second sequence (5 beats, about 9 seconds each)

The sequence opens with wide global scale and narrows to the Philippines, so it hands off to the product demo.

| Beat | Headline on screen | Stat source | Caption (max 12 words) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guardian: "Akiya houses: why Japan has nine million empty homes" | S1 (MOJ) | Japan: unregistered inheritances cause about two-thirds of owner-unknown land. |
| 2 | Business Standard: "Unclaimed deposits with banks rise 26% to Rs 78,213 crore: RBI report" | S8 (RBI) | India: ₹78,213 crore unclaimed in banks, some awaiting deceased depositors' heirs. |
| 3 | KOKH Fox 25: "Oklahomans connect with $260M in life insurance…" | S5 (NAIC) | US: nearly $17 billion in lost life insurance matched since 2016. |
| 4 | Bernama: "RM452 Mln Deferred Funds Held In Trust…" (or The Star/ANN RM90bil headline) | S2 (NRES), S3 | Malaysia: 46,707 small-estate cases unresolved; RM90 billion estimated frozen. |
| 5 | BusinessWorld: "BIR: No deadline for amnesty seekers to submit proof of estate settlement" | S4 (DOF/PIA) | Philippines: government pushes amnesty extension to unlock idle inherited assets. |

On-screen labelling rules:
- Beat 4: show "estimated, unaudited" in small text under RM90 billion. The 46,707 figure is official.
- Beat 2: say "some" or "including". Do not imply the whole amount is inheritance money.
- Each beat: show outlet name and date. Put the primary source in the credits or a lower-third.

Alternates: Singapore S6 ("S$184m in deceased members' CPF unclaimed with Public Trustee"), and UK #7 (134% rise in year-long probate cases, 2020–2023). If the UK item is used, caption it with the year range, because current waits are about 5 weeks.
