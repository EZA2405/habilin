# Philippine legal, regulatory and institutional feasibility evidence

Research checked **20 October 2026, Asia/Manila**. General product-feasibility research, not an opinion on an individual estate. Evidence labels: **official rule/statute** establishes a legal requirement; **official procedure** establishes a published process; **official opinion** is fact-dependent guidance; **official implementation** establishes an available service, not Habilin integration or actual service quality. Sources and exact provisions are indexed below.

## Determination

**A professional-supervised preparation and case-progress system is credible. An unattended agent that can legally complete an estate settlement or guarantee institutional acceptance is unsupported.** Tax arithmetic, document completeness checks and approval routing are software problems; lawful representation, evidential sufficiency, ownership disputes, certified statements, notarization, agency decisions and bank authorization remain separate requirements. Current project evidence is a synthetic restricted workflow; no live BIR/LRA/bank/PSA access, issued credential, professional validation or accepted real return has been demonstrated.

**eCAR** means **electronic Certificate Authorizing Registration**: BIR's tax clearance needed for the subsequent registration of relevant property transfers. **Title transfer** is the separate Registry of Deeds process that records the change and issues the relevant title. A filed return, paid tax or even an eCAR does not alone establish a newly registered title. The prototype should keep all four stages separate: filing, payment, eCAR and registration. [PH04](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20NO.%2033-2026.pdf), [PH09](https://lra.gov.ph/frequently-asked-questions/).

## What changed in current sources

- **Do not rely on the old Form1801 venue paragraph alone.** RA11976 amended NIRC90(D)/91, and RR4-2024 introduced electronic filing as the norm. RMC75-2026 Q3–4 clarifies current ONETT channels and the estate eCAR office linked to estate TIN registration. [PH02](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph//thebookshelf//showdocs/2/96948), [PH03](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%204-2024%20%28final%29.pdf), [PH05](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf).
- **Current form software matters.** RMC104-2026 is dated24September and posted29September2026. Its eBIRForms7.9.6.2 release corrects issues affecting **1801v2018**. Earlier7.9.6.0/.1 remain operational except forms affected by the corrections. A generic “Form1801 supported” claim is inadequate without version validation. [PH06](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%20104-2026.pdf).
- **eNotarization is a current institutional path.** SC's15October2026 announcement scheduled nationwide rollout19October; its public portal now links Twala, QuanbyLegal and NotarizeIT. This remains a commissioned human notary using an accredited facility; software approval or an app OTP is not notarization. SC-LRA acceptance guidelines also exist. [PH10](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/11/98644), [PH11](https://sc.judiciary.gov.ph/sc-strengthens-digital-transformation-through-enotarization-partnership-with-lra-and-sec/), [PH12](https://sc.judiciary.gov.ph/enotarization/registration-portal/).
- **Do not advertise newly reopened estate amnesty.** RMC33-2026 still refers to16June2025 as the availment deadline. It allows later settlement proof for prior availers but requires that proof for eCAR. A pending extension bill is not enactment. No later enacted reopening was verified. Also, a death in2026 falls outside RA11956's death-date coverage even if a new administrative deadline were discussed. [PH04](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20NO.%2033-2026.pdf), [PH24](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph//thebookshelf//showdocs/2/96664), [PH25](https://legacy.senate.gov.ph/lis/bill_res.aspx?congress=20&q=SBN-1866).

## Important limits for legal and tax claims

The standard ordinary post2018 regime is established; applicability is not established by arithmetic. RR12-2018 sections3–6 tie law and valuation to death, include residents/citizens' relevant estate interests, distinguish nonresident aliens and allow deductions subject to conditions. An “unmarried” flag does not prove absence of a qualifying family home, claims or other assets. The fixture's PHP150,000 is conditional on PHP15m accepted value ×50% legally established interest, PHP5m standard deduction and no other relevant adjustments. It is not a general estimator for arbitrary estates. [PH01](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No%2012-2018.pdf).

RA12001 sections18/29 introduces approved schedules of market values and transitional rules. For a real case, record locality, effective SMV/zonal source, valuation date and professional determination of the applicable transition. Do not tell users the historic max(zonal,assessor) formula necessarily remains sufficient everywhere. [PH26](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/97502).

One year after death remains the ordinary return deadline; extensions, installments and special relief are distinct approvals. Software should show a baseline with rule version and preserve official relief evidence. No current penalty engine or case-specific extension assessment is validated here. [PH07](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/1801%20GL%20%20final_rev.pdf).

Rule74's no-will/no-debt route is conditional, not equivalent to “uncontested.” Participation, notice, publication and protection of claims matter. LRA's requirements specifically identify court approval where minors are involved and final court orders for judicial settlement. The agent must route these cases to counsel rather than decide they are harmless exceptions. A will cannot bypass probate merely because everyone agrees. [PH13](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/63437), [PH09](https://lra.gov.ph/frequently-asked-questions/), [PH14](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/69776).

An internal `owner` credential and OTP show an app authorization decision. They do not establish that one heir can bind all heirs, that an estate representative is duly empowered, that the payer controls the bank account or that a CPA/lawyer holds an active professional or tax-agent credential. Production needs reviewed authority evidence, scope, expiry/revocation, signer identity and the specific action it permits. [PH08](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/1801_Jan%202018%20ENCS.pdf), [PH05](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2075-2026_Redacted.pdf).

## Privacy and trustworthiness findings

**Lawful purpose is necessary; “the family consented” is insufficient by itself.** DPA sections3(l),12/13,17,20/21 distinguish sensitive information, lawful bases, rights and accountability. Tax returns, government identifiers and other sensitive fields warrant strict controls. Identify the law practice/accountancy firm's controller role and Habilin/model/hosting processor roles according to actual purposes, not just contractual labels. Document a lawful basis per processing activity; section12 legitimate interests alone does not resolve section13 sensitive-data requirements. [PH16](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/).

There is unusually direct estate guidance: NPC Opinion2025-012 recognizes necessary Registry of Deeds access for establishing/exercising estate-settlement legal claims even before litigation. It remains subject to access rules and the supplied facts, not a universal API entitlement. Opinion2025-008 requires support for claimed purpose, case-by-case custodian evaluation and proportionality for death records; it says inter-controller data-sharing agreements are not invariably mandatory, while remaining good practice. Do not confuse that with outsourced-processing obligations. [PH20](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NPC-Advisory-Opinion-No.-2025-012-redacted.pdf), [PH21](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NPC-Advisory-Opinion-No.-2025-008.pdf).

**Contradictory deceased-data material must be disclosed.** NPC's formal2018-037 opinion treats deceased individuals as data subjects and invokes section17 transmissible rights. A February2026 article hosted by NPC argues deceased data generally lacks coverage, but expressly states it is the author's view, not NPC's official position. Do not represent that article as a superseding rule. Living heirs', representatives' and other parties' data are present regardless; apply protection to the whole case and obtain counsel/NPC clarification if the distinction affects the product. [PH18](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/AONo.2018-037.pdf), [PH19](https://privacy.gov.ph/til-death-do-we-part/).

NPC2024-04 sections1–3 applies privacy obligations across AI development, testing and deployment; addresses accuracy/minimization, accountability, meaningful human intervention for risky decisions and contestability. It also cautions against overstating AI involvement. Describing a classifier plus fixed planner accurately is preferable to claiming autonomous legal judgment. [PH17](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Advisory-2024.12.19-Guidelines-on-Artificial-Intelligence-w-SGD.pdf).

NPC2023-06 requires a privacy impact assessment and risk-based controls. The current synthetic local demo is not evidence of real-data compliance. Before accepting real records, implement data inventory, least-privilege case access, encryption/key management, redaction of prompts/logs, retention/deletion and legal-hold policy, backup restoration, breach response, processor due diligence and applicable DPO/system-registration assessment. Cross-border hosting is not automatically prohibited; controller accountability and comparable protection remain, and NPC2024-01 provides model contractual clauses. Neither cloud selection nor a contract alone establishes effective security. [PH27](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NPC-Circular-Repeal-16-01-Signed.pdf), [PH28](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Published-NPC-Advisory-No.-2024-01-Contractual-Clauses-for-Cross-Border-Transfers_30May24.pdf).

## Institutional feasibility and competition

The official eONETT public landing currently describes **sales/donations**. Generic ONETT rules discussing estates do not prove an enabled estate module or public developer API. BIR's Taxpayer Portal is currently advertised for Large taxpayers, not every estate. Those are access limits to investigate; failure to find a public API is not proof integration is impossible. Avoid screen-scraping accounts or reusing credentials without written channel permission. [PH29](https://eonett.bir.gov.ph/), [PH30](https://www.bir.gov.ph/).

The strongest directly verified local precedent is BIR's March2026 certification list: **WYVERN-ONETT supports certified Form1801**. Tax software is therefore already real and the competitive baseline includes certified electronic filing. That does not independently verify Wyvern's accuracy, adoption, APIs or agent behavior. Habilin must differentiate through measured evidence reconciliation and controlled progress across institutions, not “first software to file estate tax.” [PH22](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2020-2026%20Annex%20A%20(6).pdf).

Published processing targets are not measured outcomes. RMO12-2025 classifies estate computation as highly technical (20working days) and eCAR separately (7working days) after complete requirements. Build human queues, missing-item tracking and durable pause/resume. A BIR August2026 bulletin documents an actual eBIRForms outage and specified temporary relief; it does not establish an ongoing October outage or a universal deadline extension. [PH15](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMO%20No.%2012-2025.pdf), [PH31](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/Bank%20Bulletin%20No.%202026-10_redacted_compressed.pdf).

## Highest-value validation and professional questions

1. **Lawyer+CPA supported-case review:** independently assess three synthetic packets: valid simple case, unknown co-owner/minor, conflicting share/valuation. Require written accept/block reasons and source provisions. Ask which authority proves each signer's power, how revocation is handled, which documents must be original/certified and which exclusions are mandatory.
2. **Current1801 comparison:** CPA hand-calculates fixture; compare all official form fields and attachments against current7.9.6.2. Include PHP5m threshold edge, fractional share, valuation effective date and a family-home ambiguity. Acceptance criterion: no unsupported deduction/ownership assumption silently proceeds.
3. **Institution discovery before implementation:** obtain permitted integration documentation from a certified1801 TSP, a bank/AAB provider and relevant RDO. Verify credentials, form support, filing evidence, payment reference fields, timeout/status handling, duplicate suppression and commercial/API terms. A meeting or vendor promise is weaker than a sandbox contract test.
4. **Authority negatives:** expired SPA, wrong estate, single heir purporting to approve everyone's shares, substituted signer, stale packet, missing CPA credential and revoked payer authority. Every invalid case must block consequential actions even through direct tool calls.
5. **Independent-evidence demo:** accepted return receipt, tax payment confirmation, eCAR and RD title must be separate inputs. Wrong TIN, amount, form, period, reference or estate must not complete the stage. Mark manually verified evidence with verifier/time; never relabel a screenshot as independently authenticated API evidence.
6. **Privacy gate:** DPO/counsel-reviewed PIA and controller/processor map; verify minimized provider payload, deletion/backups, access isolation and breach drill before any real estate data.

Defensible judge response: “The regulations permit electronic filing and payment through authorized channels. We demonstrate bounded workflow controls in a simulation. We still need verified legal authority, professional review and an approved institutional connection before real execution.”
