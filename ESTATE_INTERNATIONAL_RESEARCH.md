# Estate problems beyond the Philippines

Research date: 20 October 2026 (Asia/Manila). Scope: primary-source checks in England and Wales, Singapore, Japan, and California, plus US insurance discovery. This investigates the financial workflow, not the legal validity of a particular will. No customer interviews, institutional access, or baseline-agent benchmark was performed. Pamana is inspiration, not a specification. No blockchain.

## Finding

The international evidence supports a real problem, but not a universal story that estates take ten years or that every month without a will automatically increases tax. The recurring problems are **assets whose ownership or beneficiary instructions are unclear, money unavailable when obligations become due, and evidence needed to demonstrate an estate can safely proceed**. Planning before death and administration after death are different products.

Generic will generation, conversational intake, document vaults, checklists, reminders, and account closure already have strong official and commercial alternatives. No defensible product advantage has yet been established. Domain rules plus an audit log are useful engineering; they are not a moat without demonstrated buyer value and a comparison against an agent with the same tools.

## Four jurisdiction checks

### 1. England and Wales: tax liquidity can precede asset access

Inheritance Tax is generally due at the end of the sixth month after death, with interest for late payment. Payment towards tax is normally needed before a grant. This creates a potential liquidity and sequencing issue, rather than proof that borrowing is always necessary. [HMRC payment guidance](https://www.gov.uk/paying-inheritance-tax/overview)

Existing remedies matter: participating banks can pay HMRC directly from deceased customers' accounts; some illiquid assets qualify for annual instalments over ten years; HMRC allows applications to postpone payment in appropriate cases. Since April 2024, representatives are not expected to seek commercial loans before applying for a grant on credit. An app recommending a loan without checking these options would be poor advice. [Direct payment](https://www.gov.uk/paying-inheritance-tax/deceaseds-bank-account), [instalments](https://www.gov.uk/paying-inheritance-tax/yearly-instalments), [HMRC manual, updated April 2026](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm05122)

Do not claim a universal probate backlog. Official April–June 2026 figures report mean application-to-grant times of approximately five weeks for probate, 22 weeks for letters of administration with a will, and ten weeks without a will. These are different grant types, not a causal experiment about having a will, and do not measure the full estate-settlement journey. [Ministry of Justice, published 24 September 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/family-court-statistics-quarterly-april-to-june-2026/family-court-statistics-quarterly-april-to-june-2026)

Practical research lead: a professional's tax-liquidity evidence check. Buyer willingness and an unmet gap remain unverified.

### 2. Singapore: a will does not govern every financial asset

Singapore abolished estate duty for deaths on or after 15 February 2008. Therefore a Philippine estate-tax escalation pitch cannot simply be exported there. [IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/other-taxes/estate-duty/estate-duty/)

CPF savings are outside the estate and are not controlled by a will. Without a valid nomination, CPF Board says identifying and verifying beneficiaries through the Public Trustee can take up to six months and incur fees. These are conditional consequences, not a claim that every CPF payout takes six months. [CPF will/nominations](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/i-have-already-made-a-will-do-i-still-need-to-make-a-cpf-nomination-for-my-cpf-savings), [CPF on death](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-happens-to-a-member-s-cpf-savings-when-he-passes-away)

There is an important asset boundary: property bought with CPF, CPF Investment Scheme investments, and Dependants' Protection Scheme payouts are not covered by the CPF nomination. This creates a concrete consistency-check problem, but an official portal already handles nominations and eligible nominees already receive automatic disbursement. A reminder tool would add little. [Coverage table](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-are-the-cpf-monies-and-assets-of-a-deceased-member-covered-under-a-cpf-nomination), [automatic disbursement](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/account-services/cpf-asset-management/on-your-passing-away)

For probate, the court estimates approximately two to three months depending on complexity; an official self-service route is available for eligible sole executors and estates estimated at no more than S$2 million. The original physical will still needs verification. [Court filing guidance](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/family/how-to-file-grant-of-probate), [Probate eService](https://www.judiciary.gov.sg/services/e-platforms/probate-eservice)

Practical research lead: adviser-facing reconciliation of intended recipients against actual, separately maintained asset instructions. We have no CPF integration and cannot claim to verify nomination status merely from a user's statement.

### 3. Japan: property title continuity and tax operate on different clocks

The Ministry of Justice identifies long-unregistered inheritances as a cause of owner-unknown land that obstructs transactions and public works. Inheritance registration became mandatory on 1 April 2024, normally within three years of learning of inherited property; older unregistered inheritances must be registered by 31 March 2027. Failure without justification can attract a civil fine up to ¥100,000. An individual declaration route can address the registration obligation in some unresolved divisions, but does not publish the resulting property rights. Software cannot settle the family disagreement itself. [MOJ English guidance](https://www.moj.go.jp/EN/MINJI/m_minji07_00004.html), [MOJ problem description](https://www.moj.go.jp/MINJI/minji05_00599.html)

Separately, where inheritance-tax filing is required, NTA states filing and payment are due within ten months from the day following notification of the decedent's death. Registration timing does not reset the valuation date or tax obligation. This is not a ten-month deadline on every estate irrespective of exemption. [NTA English framework](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/others/02/15001.htm), [NTA Japanese clarification, law as of April 2026](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/sozoku/4205_qa.htm)

Practical research lead: professional review of broken title chains and tax/liquidity dependencies. Japanese language, registry access, family-record access, and expert rule maintenance make a real deployment unsuitable for a one-day cram. A synthetic example is feasible but proves little about access.

### 4. California / US insurance discovery: knowing the asset exists is separate from authority to obtain it

California Courts instruct people to inventory not just values but ownership form, named beneficiaries, liabilities, and date-of-death interests. Some assets can pass without formal probate; a will alone does not determine the route. Do not treat all property as a single probate pool. [California Courts inventory guidance](https://www.selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/probate/inventory-estimate-value), [transfer routes](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/fa/node/1425)

For a county-specific timing check, Tulare County's July 2026 rules require a final-distribution judgment or administration-status report within twelve months after letters, or eighteen where a federal estate-tax return is required. These are court reporting requirements, not observed average completion times or a promise of completion. [Tulare rules, chapter 10](https://www.tulare.courts.ca.gov/system/files/local-rules/remediated-local-rules-effective-70126.pdf)

Separately, a US-wide regulator-operated insurance locator establishes that missing policies are a real financial issue. NAIC reported more than 780,000 life-policy/annuity matches totaling approximately $16.99 billion through July 2026. These are cumulative matched benefits since the service launched, not the annual market opportunity or verified disbursements. Searches can take 90 business days or longer, and silence can mean no match, lack of beneficiary status, or lack of authority. A search result cannot safely be labelled 'no policy exists.' [NAIC, 3 September 2026](https://content.naic.org/article/naic-life-insurance-policy-locator-tool-helps-match-consumers-more-16-billion-lost-and-unclaimed)

Practical research lead: authority-aware tracking of outstanding recovery evidence. A free existing locator defeats a pitch consisting only of searching for policies.

## Narrow opportunities worth testing, ranked for a one-day synthetic demo

These are proposals/inferences from the verified workflows. None is validated demand or demonstrated competitive advantage.

### 1. Estate liquidity and release check

- **User/buyer hypothesis:** estate accountant, small probate practice, or trust administrator reviewing many cases.
- **Job:** show whether documented, accessible funds cover professionally determined obligations before an optional beneficiary distribution. Exclude funds passing directly to nominees; preserve unconfirmed liabilities as unresolved rather than inventing a zero.
- **Minimum engine:** integer money ledger, explicit asset-access states, professional-entered tax/reserve obligations, dated confirmations, and server-enforced holds. It does not interpret a disputed will or decide legal priority unaided.
- **One-day story:** synthetic estate has ample headline value but mostly property; agent proposes a payout; engine blocks it because liquid funds are insufficient and a liability is unconfirmed. A revised official confirmation changes the scenario. Prompt injection cannot erase the hold or redirect payment.
- **Beyond a general agent:** only if tested controls and reconciliation prevent consequential errors under adversarial/revised inputs. A general agent can call the same engine; that is compatible with a product but means the chat UI is not its advantage.
- **Access reality:** no bank, tax-authority or court integration verified. Synthetic confirmations must be labelled simulated. Current Estateably distribution functionality may already be sufficient.
- **Kill criterion:** an existing platform or spreadsheet plus general agent handles the same exception cases safely at comparable effort, or no professional identifies a costly failure left unresolved.

### 2. Beneficiary-instruction consistency review

- **Buyer hypothesis:** adviser or insurer reviewing clients after life changes.
- **Job:** compare a client's stated wishes with dated asset-specific nomination/title records and identify missing or contradictory evidence without claiming that wishes supersede legal instructions.
- **Minimum engine:** jurisdiction/asset routing table, evidence status and date, identity matching, explicit conflicting-record state. No automatic nominee changes.
- **One-day story:** Singapore will assigns everything to a child, while supplied CPF nomination names another person; CPFIS holdings are incorrectly grouped with CPF balances. System separates all three and requests authoritative confirmation before declaring the plan aligned.
- **Beyond a general agent:** maintained asset rules and verified change completion across institutions could matter; identifying a contradiction in uploaded PDFs alone probably does not clear the user's filter.
- **Access reality:** no nomination/title feeds verified. Existing advisers and official CPF tools are the baseline.
- **Kill criterion:** value disappears when the general agent receives the same documents and rules, or institutions cannot provide consented evidence of current status.

### 3. Claim-to-cash reconciliation for estate benefits

- **Buyer hypothesis:** insurance claims-support or professional executor team.
- **Job:** preserve distinctions between discovery requested, eligible claimant confirmed, claim accepted and money actually received. Partial payment must not close the claim; no-response searches remain unresolved.
- **Minimum engine:** per-institution claim state machine, duplicate/partial receipt matching, authority scopes and approved destination checks.
- **One-day story:** three synthetic policies, one duplicate letter, one partial payment and a malicious email requesting a new destination. Only ledger-confirmed receipts reduce the receivable; unauthorized details cannot change it.
- **Beyond a general agent:** reliable institutional connections and measured exception handling are necessary; a tracker or email drafter alone fails.
- **Access reality:** NAIC already provides discovery, Empathy already supports benefits claims. No insurer connection verified.
- **Kill criterion:** existing claims platforms close this workflow, or we cannot establish access beyond documents the agent can already read.

### 4. Inherited-property transfer readiness

- **Buyer hypothesis:** property/conveyancing practice serving inherited-property sales.
- **Job:** detect missing links between current title, deceased owner, documented succession steps and proposed seller before the sale starts.
- **Minimum engine:** evidence/dependency graph and a constrained jurisdiction-specific ruleset, reviewed by a qualified practitioner. This is readiness checking, not adjudicating ownership.
- **One-day story:** synthetic title remains in a grandparent's name while a grandchild attempts sale; engine identifies an unverified intermediate estate rather than generating a false ready-to-transfer packet.
- **Beyond a general agent:** verified registry access, reliable linkage and execution acceptance would be the value. A family-tree generator is readily substituted.
- **Access reality:** no registry/family-record access or professional rule validation. Highest legal and acceptance risk of these four.
- **Kill criterion:** rules need bespoke legal reasoning for nearly every case, or the bottleneck is heir dispute/court time that software cannot change.

## Decision constraint

No candidate passes the user's substitution filter on demonstrated evidence. The ordering is a priority for testing, not an approved build or proven market opportunity. Current AGENTS.md hard-blocks personal-account payouts, including general heir transfers. All scenarios here are read-only proposed-release checks, rejection tests and sandbox reviewer decisions; no beneficiary payout is executed or authorized. An approved internal review does not grant bank, court or tax authority.

If a concept is later selected, constrain any prototype to **one jurisdiction, one asset class and one measurable failure**, sized to the hours actually left before submission. A bounded candidate for testing is an estate-liquidity/release check with synthetic authority records, professional-entered obligations and direct bypass tests. It demonstrates a finance and security engine, but overlaps established products and still needs real buyer evidence. Do not pitch it as a proven opportunity or a replacement for lawyers.

Compare against Hermes/OpenClaw given the same statements, rules and institution tools: correct balances, stale-confirmation rejection, unknown-status preservation, duplicate/partial handling, blocked unauthorized payouts and reviewer effort. If the difference comes solely from hand-feeding our demo special information, the comparison is invalid.

Skipped: jurisdiction-wide market sizing, customer interviews, current service quotations, production integrations, legal review and agent baseline execution. Risk: strong public evidence of estate friction does not prove our chosen workflow is underserved or that it can be reliably deployed.
